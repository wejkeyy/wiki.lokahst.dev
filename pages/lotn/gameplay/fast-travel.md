Players can save up to **3** personal fast-travel points.

## Saving a Point

Open `/travel`, choose an empty slot, and save the solid block currently beneath the player. The destination must have safe ground, two passable body spaces, and remain inside the world border.

## Travelling

A player cannot fast travel while:

- dead
- sleeping
- riding an entity
- in combat
- standing in a world where LotN is disabled

The saved destination must still exist, remain enabled, and pass the safety check. Fast travel has departure and arrival visual effects and can progress `TRAVEL` quest objectives.

## Managing Points

Each slot can be selected, overwritten through the menu flow, or deleted. Points are stored in the player profile and mirrored in persistent player data for migration compatibility.
