Each character can save up to three Fast Travel Points.

## Unlocks

| Wayfaring level | Unlock |
|---|---|
| 1 | First Fast Travel Point |
| 2 | Second Fast Travel Point |
| 3 | Third Fast Travel Point |

## Saving a Point

To save a point, the player must stand on a solid, safe block with two empty blocks above it. LotN saves the exact position and world.

## Default Timing

- Base preparation: `5` seconds
- Base cooldown: `30` seconds
- Nearby hostile radius: `12` blocks
- Recent damage lock: `8` seconds

## Travel Is Blocked When

- The player is dead, sleeping, riding, falling, or not grounded.
- The player is in combat or was damaged recently.
- A hostile mob is too close.
- Carry weight is at or above 100%.
- The destination or world is unavailable.
- The cooldown is active.
- The player moves or takes damage during preparation.

Movement does not grant Wayfaring XP for five seconds after arrival, so the teleport cannot be used to farm travel XP.
