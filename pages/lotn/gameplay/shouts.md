Shouts are enabled by default and begin with `3` unlocked words per shout in EA13.

## Activation

Double-tap Shift within `350` milliseconds to use the selected shout. The Shout menu is available from the main LotN interface.

| Shout | Words | Effect | Magicka | Cooldown seconds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unrelenting Force | Fus Ro Dah | Pushes, damages, and staggers enemies | 25 | 12 |
| Whirlwind Sprint | Wuld Nah Kest | Launches the player forward and stops before unsafe blocks | 25 | 10 |
| Fire Breath | Yol Toor Shul | Damages and ignites enemies in a cone | 30 | 18 |
| Become Ethereal | Feim Zii Gron | Prevents incoming damage while disabling offensive actions | 35 | 45 |
| Storm Call | Strun Bah Qo | Starts a thunderstorm and strikes nearby enemies | 60 | 1200 |
| Aura Whisper | Laas Yah Nir | Reveals nearby mobs through walls | 30 | 300 |
| Battle Cry | Kaan Drem Ov | Forces nearby mobs to stop fighting temporarily | 50 | 3600 |
| Clear Skies | Lok Vah Koor | Stops rain, thunder, and active Storm Call | 20 | 300 |
| Dragon Aspect | Mul Qah Diiv | Boosts worn armor rating for five minutes | 75 | 3600 |

## Word Scaling

Unrelenting Force uses one configured effect set, while Whirlwind Sprint, Fire Breath, Become Ethereal, Aura Whisper, and Battle Cry scale range or duration with the number of unlocked words.

## Safety and Target Rules

Default offensive shouts do not affect players or tamed animals. Line-of-sight checks are enabled where appropriate. Become Ethereal blocks offensive actions for its duration, and Whirlwind Sprint checks the travel path before moving the player.

Race modifiers can change shout cooldowns.
