When the Fishing system is enabled, successful catches are replaced with LotN fish, junk, or treasure rolls.

## Catch Defaults

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Catch cooldown | 20 ticks |
| Base catch chance | 85% |
| Junk chance | 10% |
| Treasure chance | 3% |
| Base overall XP | 0.35 |
| Base Fishing XP | 4.0 |

## Fishdex

The Fishdex records every species the player has caught. It shows discovered fish, catches per species, and total catches. Player data also tracks total sale value, the most expensive catch, and the rarest catch.

## Selling Fish

Open Fish Exchange from `/lotn` to sell all LotN fish in the inventory. Payments use Vault.
