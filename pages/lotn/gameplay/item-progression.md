---
title: Item Progression
description: Weapon and armor levels
updated: August 1, 2026
---

Registered weapons and armor can gain their own levels. Item XP is stored directly on the equipment, and the system is enabled by default.

| Item type | XP source | XP per action | XP per item level | Maximum level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weapon | Kills | 1.0 | 1.0 | 100 |
| Armor | Damage received while worn | 1.0 | 10.0 | 100 |
