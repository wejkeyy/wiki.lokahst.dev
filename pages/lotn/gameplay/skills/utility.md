Utility skills cover exploration and trading.

## Wayfaring

Explore normally to unlock Fast Travel Points.

**Maximum level:** `3`

**XP sources:** Walking and sprinting; new biomes and dimensions; sleeping in new beds; exploration milestones; reaching saved destinations without teleporting.

| Level | Reward | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fast Travel I | Unlock Fast Travel and one Fast Travel Point |
| 2 | Fast Travel II | Unlock a second Fast Travel Point |
| 3 | Fast Travel III | Unlock a third and final Fast Travel Point |

## Trading

Buy and sell items through LotN trading systems.

**Maximum level:** `10`

**XP sources:** Value of items bought and sold; different merchants; item rarity; crafted goods; larger trades.

| Level | Reward | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trader I | Tracked item sales are worth 2% more |
| 2 | Customer I | Tracked purchases cost 1% less |
| 3 | Market Sense | Show whether a price is below, equal to, or above normal |
| 4 | Trader II | Total item sale bonus: 4% |
| 5 | Bulk Trader | Stacks of at least 16 get +2% sale value |
| 6 | Customer II | Total purchase discount: 2% |
| 7 | Appraiser | Show base value, quality, enchantment value, and final estimate |
| 8 | Trusted Customer | Unlock rotating special merchant offers |
| 9 | Expert Trader | +8% sale value and 3% lower purchase prices |
| 10 | Master Trader | +10% sale value, 5% lower purchase prices, and master offers |
