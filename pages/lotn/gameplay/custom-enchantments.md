## Finding Enchanted Gear

Eligible scaled mobs may drop gear containing a LotN enchantment.

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Base drop chance | 3.5% |
| Additional chance per mob level | 0.035% |
| Maximum chance | 12% |

The Smithing perk **Eye Glass** enables qualifying enchanted LotN gear drops.

## Learning Enchantments

Use a grindstone to disenchant an item containing a LotN enchantment. The enchantment is added permanently to the character's known enchantments and grants `12.0` Enchanting XP.

## Applying Enchantments

Open `/enchants`, hold a compatible item in the main hand or on the cursor, and click a learned enchantment. Applying one grants `3.0` Enchanting XP.

Vanilla enchanting is disabled by default through `world-settings.disable-vanilla-enchanting`.
