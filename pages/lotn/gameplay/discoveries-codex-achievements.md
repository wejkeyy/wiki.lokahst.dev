## Discoveries

LotN automatically records new biomes, dimensions, and exploration milestones. Discovery checks use `32`-block cells.

| Discovery | Default overall XP |
|---|---|
| New biome | 0.5 |
| New dimension | 3.0 |
| Exploration milestone | 1.5 |

Milestones include reaching Y `180` and descending below Y `-20`.
