## Discoveries

LotN records newly visited biomes and dimensions, along with exploration milestones from the config.

| Discovery | Default overall XP |
|---|---|
| New biome | 0.5 |
| New dimension | 3.0 |
| Exploration milestone | 1.5 |

The default milestones are reaching Y `180` and descending below Y `-20`.

## Codex Categories

| Category | ID | Icon |
|---|---|---|
| Fish | `fish` | `COD` |

Entries are added to the Codex as players catch fish, explore, discover enchantments, finish quests, defeat elite enemies, and earn achievements.
