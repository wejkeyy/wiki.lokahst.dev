A race is selected after the character name and cannot be changed through normal gameplay.

| Race | Bonuses | Penalties |
|---|---|---|
| Breton | +10% One-Handed damage; +10 Magicka; +5% shield effectiveness | -15% smithing quality |
| High Elf | +20 Magicka; +15% Magicka regeneration | -15% Stamina regeneration; +15% shout cooldown |
| Wood Elf | +15% bow/crossbow damage; poison immunity; +5% Stamina regeneration | -12% One-Handed damage |
| Nord | +8% health regeneration; +8% carry weight | -5% bow/crossbow damage |
| Imperial | +13% gold from selling; 7% better buying prices; +5% smithing quality | -14% Stamina regeneration |

## Notes

- Racial multipliers stack with compatible attributes, perks, equipment, and potion effects.
- Wood Elves are immune to LotN poison effects.
- High Elves pay longer shout cooldowns in exchange for stronger Magicka stats.
- Imperial economy bonuses only matter when Vault and an economy provider are active.
