A race cannot be changed through normal gameplay. Each one gives a few bonuses and penalties to combat, crafting, economy, regeneration, or survival.

| Race | Bonuses | Penalties |
|---|---|---|
| Breton | +10% one-handed damage; +10 Magicka; +5% shield effectiveness | -15% tempering quality |
| High Elf | +20 Magicka; +15% Magicka regeneration | -15% Stamina regeneration; shout cooldowns are 15% longer |
| Wood Elf | +15% ranged damage; +5% Stamina regeneration; poison immunity | -12% one-handed damage |
| Nord | +8% Health regeneration; +8% carry capacity | -5% ranged damage |
| Imperial | +5% tempering quality; +13% sale value; 7% cheaper buying prices | -14% Stamina regeneration |
