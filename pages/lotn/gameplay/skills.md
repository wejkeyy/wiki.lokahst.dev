Skills begin at level **15** and can reach level **100**.

![Skill selection](assets/skill-selection.png)

| Skill | Category | How it is used | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| One-Handed | Combat | Deal real damage with swords and other configured one-handed weapons. | Enabled |
| Archery | Combat | Damage valid enemies with bows and crossbows. | Enabled |
| Block | Combat | Prevent incoming combat damage with a raised shield. | Enabled |
| Heavy Armor | Protection | Survive combat while wearing heavy armor. | Enabled |
| Light Armor | Protection | Survive combat while wearing light armor. | Enabled |
| Sneak | Protection | Land valid attacks while sneaking. | Enabled |
| Fishing | Professions | Catch fish, treasure, and legendary northern creatures. | Enabled |
| Alchemy | Crafting | Study ingredients and brew legitimate LOTN mixtures. | Enabled |
| Enchanting | Crafting | Learn and apply LOTN enchantments. | Enabled |
| Smithing | Crafting | Craft equipment and extract smelted materials. | Enabled |

## How Skill XP Is Earned

| Skill | Main XP sources |
|---|---|
| One-Handed | Valid melee damage with supported one-handed weapons |
| Archery | Valid damage with bows and crossbows, with distance and critical bonuses |
| Block | Real damage prevented by an actively raised shield |
| Heavy Armor | Combat damage survived while wearing heavy armor, plus movement familiarity |
| Light Armor | Combat damage survived while wearing light armor, plus movement familiarity |
| Sneak | Undetected attacks and stealth activity near hostile creatures |
| Fishing | Successful LotN catches |
| Alchemy | Studying ingredients and brewing valid mixtures |
| Enchanting | Learning and applying LotN enchantments |
| Smithing | Crafting equipment, smelting materials, and tempering gear |

## Anti-Exploit Rules

By default, PvP targets, friendly targets, plugin-spawned targets, environmental armor damage, and dispenser-created blocks do not produce normal skill XP. Repeated attacks on the same entity or entity type receive diminishing returns.

## Progress Feedback

The action bar can show progress at each `25%` threshold. Individual skill XP chat messages are disabled by default.
