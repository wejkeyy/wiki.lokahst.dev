## Enhanced Dragon AI

When enhanced dragon AI is enabled, the Ender Dragon gains two extra attacks.

### Fire Breath

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Chance per decision | 35% |
| Cooldown | 260 ticks |
| Windup | 24 ticks |
| Range | 45.0 blocks |
| Cone angle | 18.0 degrees |
| Damage | 7.0 |
| Fire duration | 80 ticks |

### Wing Gust

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Chance per decision | 45% |
| Cooldown | 140 ticks |
| Range | 11.0 blocks |
| Damage | 3.0 |
| Horizontal force | 1.8 |
| Vertical force | 0.35 |

A player's Fire Breath deals only `15%` damage to LotN dragons. Fire enchantments deal no damage to them by default.
