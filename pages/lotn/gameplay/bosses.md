## Enhanced Dragon AI

The Ender Dragon receives additional combat decisions when enhanced AI is enabled.

### Fire Breath

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Chance per decision | 35% |
| Cooldown | 260 ticks |
| Range | 45.0 blocks |
| Damage | 7.0 |
| Fire duration | 80 ticks |

### Wing Gust

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Chance per decision | 45% |
| Cooldown | 140 ticks |
| Range | 11.0 blocks |
| Damage | 3.0 |
| Horizontal force | 1.8 |

Player Fire Breath deals only `15%` of normal damage to dragons.
