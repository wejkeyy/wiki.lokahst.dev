## Required Plugins

- Vault
- A Vault-compatible economy provider (e.g. EssentialsX)

LotN still runs without them, but balances, money rewards, and Fish Exchange sales are disabled.

## Currency Display

LotN displays money with the `◈` symbol, including on the scoreboard and fish items.

## Sources and Uses

- Fish Exchange sells LotN fish from the player inventory.
- Quests can award Vault money when a reward is set in the config.
- The scoreboard and placeholders can show the Vault balance.
- Fishing perks, Trading perks, race bonuses, and config values can change buy and sell prices.

## Villager Trading

Villager trading is enabled by default. LotN starts these trades at a `1.4x` price multiplier, then applies Trading perks and race bonuses.

Imperials receive `1.13x` sale value and pay `0.93x` when buying. Trading perks can improve both values further.
