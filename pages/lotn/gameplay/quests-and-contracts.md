Players may keep up to **5** active quests. All accepted quests progress together, while one quest may be tracked on the HUD.

## Journal Controls

- `/journal` opens active quests.
- `/quests` opens available quests.
- `/contracts` opens repeatable notice-board contracts.
- Left-click accepts or tracks a quest.
- Right-click toggles HUD tracking for an active quest.

Repeatable quests can be selected again after their configured cooldown. EA13 includes always-available repeatable tasks so the journal does not become empty.
